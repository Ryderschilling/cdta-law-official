# CDTA College of Law — what the website still needs from the Academy

Updated 2026-09-08. Everything below is built and waiting. Nothing here is a
design question; it is all content only the Academy has.

Send answers to Ryder in any form: a document, an email, a photo of a page.
It does not need to be formatted.

---

## 1. The curriculum

The website has a curriculum page built. It is hidden until this arrives,
because a law school's published course of study is not something anyone
outside the Academy can write.

For each year of the J.D. program:

- The year (First Year, Second Year, and so on)
- Every course taught in that year, by its title
- Units or credits for each course, if the Academy assigns them
- One or two sentences describing each course, if you have them

If some years are still being finalised, send the ones that are set and the
page can go up with those.

---

## 2. Alumni

The website has an alumni page built. It is hidden until this arrives.

For each graduate the Academy wants to feature:

- Name
- The year they graduated
- What they do now, and where
- Two or three sentences in their own words about the Academy
- A photograph, if they have one they are happy to use

**Two things the Academy has to confirm for each person:**

- That they have agreed to appear on the website. A law school publishing a
  former student's name, employer and bar status is publishing about a real
  person.
- Whether they are admitted to practice, and in which state. This will only go
  on the site if the Academy confirms it in writing, because it is a claim the
  State Bar can check.

---

## 3. Statistics

The site currently publishes one number: **"Over 60%" passed the First-Year Law
Students' Examination.** It carries the required note that the FYLSX is not the
bar examination and that CDTA does not publish a General Bar Examination
passage rate.

**What is missing is the period that number covers.** A rate with no years
attached is not a statistic, and it is the one thing that has never been
supplied. Please send:

- The exact years the "Over 60%" figure covers
- Whether it is still current, or should be updated to a newer figure
- Any other figure the Academy wants published, with the same three things:
  the exact wording, the years it covers, and who published it (the Academy,
  or the State Bar)

## 6. Two things that block launch

**The tuition payment schedule.** The current site states $15,000 a year and
$60,000 total, and also a $7,000 deposit plus $1,000 a month in year one plus
$1,250 a month for 36 months, which comes to $64,000. Those two do not
reconcile. The site is not publishing a payment schedule until the Academy
says which is correct.

**The State Bar required disclosure filing**, as text or a PDF. The old site
used images of it, which cannot be read by a screen reader and cannot be found
by search.
